Borrower's Guidelines

Please read the guidelines carefully before proceeding.

Borrowing of equipment must be requested and completed at least 2–5 days before the scheduled event to ensure equipment availability and proper preparation. Borrowers must present a valid identification card and all required documents.

Eligible to Borrow

Faculty, staff, employees, students, and stakeholders (external users) may borrow equipment.

Procedures

  1. Step 1:Acknowledge the Borrowing Sports Equipment Form.
  2. Step 2:Fill out the digital Borrowing Sports Equipment Form.
  3. Step 3:Submit the completed Borrowing Sports Equipment Form for review.
  4. Step 4:Await approval from the Director, SDPO.
  5. Step 5:Wait for the release of the equipment.

Responsibilities of the Borrower

  1. 1.Use equipment responsibly and only for the stated purpose.
  2. 2.Ensure the equipment is returned on time and in good condition.
  3. 3.Report any damages or issues to SDPO personnel promptly.

Return Process

  1. 1.Return equipment on the agreed date and time.
  2. 2.The Sports Development Program Office staff will thoroughly inspect the equipment upon return to assess for any damages or loss. If the returned equipment is found to be damaged or lost, the borrower will be held accountable and required to either repair or replace the items, depending on the extent of the damage.

Personal information is used solely for equipment accountability and office operations. The system applies encryption and access controls to protect borrower data from unauthorized access.
